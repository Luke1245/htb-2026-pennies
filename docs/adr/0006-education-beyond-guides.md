# Education can go beyond the Guides

Guides stay curated from trusted sources, and Gemini does not rewrite them. Education is written from the Financial portrait: it may apply a Guide, or explain a Category, Spending habit, or Projected habit that no Guide covers, and Suggestions follow from those Educations. Limiting Education to the three Guides was rejected because those articles do not cover every spending pattern Pennies needs to explain. This supersedes ADR 0005.
